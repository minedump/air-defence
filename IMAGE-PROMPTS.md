Сгенерируйте изображение по промпту, сохраните в `/public/images/` и замените
компонент `<ImagePlaceholder number="XX" … />` на `<Image src="/images/XX.jpg" … />`.

> Dark industrial photography style, moody twilight lighting, deep charcoal-blue
> color grade (#0A0F1C shadows), subtle steel-blue accent lighting (#4C8DFF),
> high detail, wide-angle architectural shot, photorealistic, no people, no text.
